About the simulation. Signals come from textbook equations with ideal pulses, using published 3 T values: T1 white matter 970 ms, grey matter 1500 ms, CSF 4300 ms; T2 73, 88 and 2030 ms; water content 65%, 78% and 97%. The anatomy is the average MNI152 brain, so edges are blurred and a thin rim around the ventricles is classed as grey matter. Gradient echo uses TR 20 ms and TE 3 ms. The noise toggle adds the same fixed noise to every image; motion, flow and magnetic-field effects are not modelled.
Values: FSL-MRS tissue constants; CSF T1 from Rooney et al. 2007, Magn Reson Med 57:308; CSF T2 from Spijkerman et al. 2018, MAGMA 31:415. Brain template: MNI ICBM152 2009a.
